Maroyz Design.
Crafted for Confidence.

Maroyz Design is a premium Afrocentric fashion brand dedicated to celebrating women in all their glorious shapes and sizes. As an expert fashion designer, pattern drafter, and crocheter, Mary Onyekachukwu Nwaokolobia brings bespoke pieces to life with an unmatched attention to detail.

The neatness of our finishing is our signature. We specialize in styling plus-size clients to absolute perfection, ensuring our garments not only fit beautifully but reflect your inner beauty, giving you the full confidence to be the highlight at any function or event.
