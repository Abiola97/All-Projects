The AHF Identity

Founded in August 2019 by Adeleke Aishat Abosede, Aeeshnamic Home of Fashion (AHF) is a pioneering brand based in Lagos, Nigeria. We cater exclusively to women aged 18 to 65 who seek to bridge the critical gap between self-expression and social norms.

Our brand is anchored in the contrast of Black and White—representing elegance, strength, and clarity. Whether through our custom designs, ready-to-wear collections, or personalized styling advice, AHF allows you to wear your heritage with pride, authority, and pure sophistication.
