


# 📊 Business (B2C) Performance Dashboard (Excel)

A dynamic Excel-based dashboard designed to transform raw service job data into actionable business insights. This project focuses on data cleaning, regional mapping, KPI generation, and interactive reporting using Power Query, PivotTables, and advanced formulas.

---

## 📦 Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes service performance across multiple cities and regions, tracking revenue, job types, and sales rep contributions. It is built to refresh automatically when new data is added, ensuring scalability and operational efficiency.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

- Standardized date formats, names, and numerical values  
- Removed duplicates, empty rows, and irrelevant columns  
- Corrected inconsistencies in sales rep names and job types  
- Linked city names to their corresponding regions using a reference table

---

## 🗺️ Region Mapping

- Mapped each city to its correct region using a lookup table  
- Automated the merge between sales and region data via Power Query  
- Ensured dynamic updates when new cities or regions are added

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔑 KPIs

- **Total Revenue**  
- **Completed Jobs**  
- **Average Revenue per Job**  
- **Revenue per Sales Rep**  
- **Total Clients & New Clients**

### 📈 Charts & Tables

- Revenue by Region (bar chart)  
- Top 5 Sales Reps by Revenue (auto-updating)  
- Revenue by Job Type (e.g., Air Duct, Commercial Work)  
- Jobs Frequency by Day of Week  
- Revenue Over Time (line chart)  
- Jobs Completed Over Time (line chart)  
- Leads Overview by Region and Job Type

### 🎛 Filters (Slicers)

- Region  
- Job Type  
- Date Range  
- Account Name

---

## ⚠️ Conditional Logic & Alerts

- Highlighted regions where revenue is < 80% of overall average  
- Flagged sales reps contributing < 5% of total revenue  
- Used conditional formatting and calculated fields for dynamic alerts
