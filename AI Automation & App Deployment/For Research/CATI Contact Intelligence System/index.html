Privacy & Security

Phone numbers are hashed with SHA-256 before any internal processing. No phone numbers, no call data, and no respondent information is ever sent to any external server. The app reads your published Google Sheet in your browser and all computation runs locally. Your Google Sheet remains under your Google account's access controls at all times.
